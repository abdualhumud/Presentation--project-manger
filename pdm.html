Selected case studies

Three products. In each one, information changed a product decision — not just an activity log.

Rased — scaling a fleet & logistics platform

Fleet · Logistics · B2G
Context
Rased runs government fleet and logistics operations on Google Cloud — multiple entities, thousands of vehicles, strict compliance requirements.
Challenge
Operations were fragmented and partly manual. The product had to scale across entities without losing governance or compliance.
Insight
A two-part audit of 43+ local and global competitors — technical (system architecture, devices, install methods, integrations) and operational (field operations, geographic distribution, services, business models) — showed the real gaps were in government integration and operational readiness, not features.
Decision
Prioritise the roadmap around integration and operational governance first, instead of chasing feature parity. That analysis produced 6 strategic product decisions.
Execution
Aligned product, engineering, cybersecurity, operations, sales and procurement under one roadmap — a 45-person cross-functional team — holding 100% cybersecurity and data-integrity compliance throughout.
Outcome
4 products and 8 services shipped, and a 13,000+ vehicle government fleet running on the platform for the Ministry of Energy.
Insight→Decision→Product change→Outcome

Digitising government logistics operations

Regulated · Field · Governance
Context
Government logistics and field work across water and energy — tanker movements, fuel distribution, field inspection — largely manual or spread across disconnected tools.
Problem
No reliable operational visibility. Compliance and governance were hard to enforce, and workflows lived on paper.
Insight
Mapping the actual field workflow — not just vehicle positions — showed the real gap was structured operations and regulatory data, not GPS tracking.
Decision
Build products around operational workflows, entitlements and governance — with government integrations — rather than a tracking dashboard.
Solution
Ministry of Energy: a fleet & fuel platform (13,000+ vehicles) with a fuel-expense wallet, station fuel-order management, and smart-nozzle verification with a hardware partner. Saudi Water Authority: 3 strategic projects for tanker logistics governance across potable, non-potable and Jeddah municipality sewage use-cases. MEWA “Hafiz”: a field-inspection product for daily tasks, reporting and field workflow.
Impact
Fragmented, manual operations became structured digital products the entities use daily — with 100% cybersecurity and data-integrity compliance.
Context→Problem→Insight→Decision→Solution→Impact

Mojaz — from customer insight to market growth

Customer · Market · B2C
Context
A consumer automotive platform entering a competitive market and needing real adoption, not just installs.
Challenge
Break into the market and turn interest into active usage.
Insight
Behavioural analysis of 800+ customers, plus a read on which channels actually moved the market (influencers and car showrooms), showed where users dropped off in onboarding.
Decision
Point go-to-market at influencer and showroom integration, and fix the onboarding drop-off — moving adoption from 12% to 38%.
Experimentation
A/B tests and short feedback loops, letting data decide what shipped next.
Outcome
25M+ impressions, +250% downloads, #1 on the Saudi App Store, and a tangible first-quarter market-share gain.
Insight→Decision→Product change→Outcome
