Case studies

Three products, three different problems

Rased — building and scaling a fleet platform

Current · B2B / B2G
Context
Rased is a fleet management product serving different operational use cases across commercial and government customers — devices in the field, daily operations, and multiple integrations.
Discovery
Customer interviews and usage data on one side; market and competitor intelligence on the other. A two-part review of 43+ local and global competitors covered the technical layer (architecture, devices, install methods, integrations) and the operational layer (field operations, coverage, services, business models).
Opportunity
The gap was not features. Competitors were weakest on integration and operational readiness, and customers were asking for services around the fleet rather than more dashboards.
Product decisions
Reprioritised the roadmap toward integrations and operational capability instead of feature parity, and opened a services layer above tracking — maintenance, expenses, driver behaviour. 6 strategic product decisions came out of this.
Launch
Each service scoped to an MVP, requirements written with engineering, operations and sales aligned on onboarding, then released in stages instead of one large launch.
Scale
The platform supports ~14K vehicles, with release cycles 20% faster than before.
Discovery→Idea→Decision→Launch→Scale

Turning government operations into digital products

Current · B2G
Problem
Government fleet and field work across energy and water ran on manual or disconnected processes — fuel handling, tanker movement, field inspection. No reliable operational picture.
Field discovery
Time with field users and entity teams to map the real workflow, the regulatory requirements behind it, and what had to integrate with existing government systems.
Product design
Built around operational workflows, entitlements and traceability rather than a tracking dashboard. Vehicle position alone answered none of the questions the entities actually had.
Innovation
Separate products for separate mandates. Ministry of Energy: government fleet and fuel operations, including a fuel-expense wallet, station fuel-order management and nozzle-level verification with a hardware partner. Saudi Water Authority: tanker logistics for potable, non-potable and Jeddah municipality sewage use-cases. MEWA — Hafiz: field inspection, daily tasks, reporting and field workflow.
Delivery
Product, engineering, operations, cybersecurity and the entity's own teams aligned on one scope, with regulatory requirements treated as part of the design rather than a review at the end.
Operational value
~13K vehicles across government fleet solutions. Manual, fragmented work became structured digital workflows with clearer operational visibility, less manual effort and traceable records.
Problem→Field discovery→Design→Innovation→Delivery→Value

Mojaz — customer insight into market growth

Previous experience · B2C
Customer data
Behavioural analysis of 800+ customers, plus a read on which channels actually moved this market.
Insight
Users were arriving but dropping out during onboarding, and the channels that converted were influencers and car showrooms rather than general awareness spend.
Product & GTM decision
Rebuilt onboarding around the drop-off point and pointed go-to-market at influencer and showroom integration.
Experiment
A/B tests and short feedback loops, with data deciding what shipped next.
Growth
Onboarding adoption moved from 12% to 38%. The product reached 540K registrations, 25M+ impressions and +250% downloads.
Customer data→Insight→Idea→Experiment→Growth
