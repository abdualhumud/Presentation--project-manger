Case studies

Rased — building and scaling a fleet platform

Fleet · B2B / B2G
Context
Rased operates in a complex B2B/B2G fleet environment: commercial customers, government requirements, IoT devices in the field, daily operations and multiple integrations.
Discovery
Customer interviews and usage data on one side, market and competitor research on the other. A two-part audit of 43+ local and global competitors covered the technical layer (architecture, devices, install methods, integrations) and the operational layer (field operations, geographic coverage, services, business models).
Opportunity
The gap was not features. Competitors were weakest on government integration and operational readiness — and customers were asking for services around the fleet, not more dashboards.
Product decisions
Reprioritised the roadmap around integrations and operational capability instead of feature parity, and opened a services layer on top of tracking — maintenance, fuel and expense, driver behaviour. 6 strategic product decisions came out of this.
Launch
Scoped each service to an MVP, wrote the requirements with engineering, aligned operations and sales on onboarding, then released in stages rather than one large launch.
Scale & performance
The platform supports ~14K vehicles, with 4 products and 8 new services launched and release cycles 20% faster.
Discovery→Innovation→Prioritisation→Launch→Scale

Government fleet & field operations

B2G · regulated environment
Problem
Government fleet and field work across water and energy ran on manual or disconnected processes — tanker movements, fuel handling, field inspection. No reliable operational picture.
Discovery
Time with field users and entity teams to map the actual workflow, the regulatory requirements behind it, and what had to integrate with existing government systems.
Product design
Built around operational workflows, entitlements and traceability rather than a tracking dashboard. Position alone answered none of the questions the entities actually had.
Innovation
Some of this was new, not digitised paper: a fuel-expense wallet and station fuel-order management, and smart-nozzle verification with a hardware partner to confirm dispensing at the source. For Saudi Water Authority, tanker logistics governance across potable, non-potable and Jeddah municipality sewage use-cases. For MEWA, “Hafiz” for field inspectors — daily tasks, reporting and field workflow.
Delivery
Product, engineering, operations, cybersecurity and the entity's own teams aligned on one scope, with regulatory compliance treated as a requirement from the start rather than a review at the end.
Scale & value
~13K vehicles across government fleet solutions. Manual and fragmented work became structured digital workflows with clearer operational visibility, less manual effort and traceable records.
Problem→Discovery→Design→Innovation→Delivery→Scale

Mojaz — customer insight into growth

B2C · growth & adoption
Context
A consumer automotive platform entering a competitive market, needing real usage rather than installs.
Customer data
Behavioural analysis of 800+ customers, plus a read on which channels actually moved this market.
Insight
Users were arriving but dropping out during onboarding, and the channels that converted were influencers and car showrooms — not general awareness spend.
Product & GTM decision
Rebuilt the onboarding flow around where users were dropping, and pointed go-to-market at influencer and showroom integration.
Experiment
A/B tests and short feedback loops, with data deciding what shipped next.
Adoption & growth
Onboarding adoption moved from 12% to 38%. The product reached 540K registrations, 25M+ impressions, +250% downloads and #1 on the Saudi App Store.
Customer data→Insight→Idea→Experiment→Adoption
